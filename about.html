Why PM English

Most English resources online are written for a global audience and skip over what students in Indian classrooms actually need — clear grammar explanations, familiar examples, and practice matched to their grade.

One class at a time

Nursery students and Class 10 students are learning completely different things. Every lesson here is written for one specific grade, not a general age range.

Plain explanations

Grammar rules are explained in simple language first, then shown in a short story or passage, so the rule makes sense before it's tested.

Growing steadily

Lessons are being added class by class. Class 3 is live first — more grades and topics are on the way.
